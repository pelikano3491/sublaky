Listen to music stored on your Android device with a cassette-inspired interface.

• Scan your music library or choose folders.
• Filter for MP3 only, or enable other supported formats.
• Search by song, artist or album.
• Shuffle playback and control music from the system media controls.
• Choose Walkman, Deck, Desktop or Fullscreen layouts.
• Watch an audio spectrum calculated from the music being played, without microphone access.

Sublaky works locally without accounts, advertising or analytics. It does not download music. Format support depends on the device and available decoders. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
